Real homes, measured to the centimetre

Rooms and whole flats people have drawn from their own tape measure, furnished with products from real shops. Open one to walk around it, see what’s in it and what it costs, or copy it as a start for yours.

My homes

Catalogue

Furniture, flooring, wallpaper and paint from shops on Nido, with their real sizes. Place any of them in your rooms; the shopping list adds up the prices and links back to the shop.

For shops
